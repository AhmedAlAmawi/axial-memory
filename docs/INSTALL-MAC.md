# Installing on a Mac

1. Download **Axial-Memory-Mac.dmg** from
   https://github.com/AhmedAlAmawi/axial-memory/releases/latest/download/Axial-Memory-Mac.dmg
2. Open the downloaded file and drag **Axial Memory** into **Applications**.
3. Open Applications and double-click **Axial Memory**. The Mac says it can't
   check the app for malicious software. Click **Done** (not Move to Trash).
4. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**. Scroll down to the line that
   says *"Axial Memory" was blocked*, click **Open Anyway**, then **Open
   Anyway** again and enter the Mac's password.

   On older macOS (before Sequoia) you can instead right-click the app in
   Applications, choose **Open**, then **Open**.
5. The app opens. You only do this once; updates install themselves from inside
   the app.

## Letting it see everything

When it searches, macOS asks a few times whether Axial Memory may look in
Desktop, Documents, Downloads and other apps' files: click **Allow**.

To skip those questions and let it into Mail attachments and other locked
places, turn on Full Disk Access:

1. In the app, click **Open Full Disk Access settings** (or open System
   Settings → Privacy & Security → Full Disk Access).
2. Click **+**, choose **Axial Memory** in Applications, and switch it on.
3. Quit and reopen Axial Memory.

After an update, macOS may ask about folders again, and Full Disk Access may
need switching off and on once.
